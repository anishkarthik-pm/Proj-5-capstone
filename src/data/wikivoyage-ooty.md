# Ooty (Udagamandalam) - Travel Guide

## Overview

Ooty, officially known as Udagamandalam, is a town in the Nilgiri Hills in Tamil Nadu, India. It is the headquarters of the Nilgiris district and is a popular hill station located at an elevation of 2,240 meters (7,350 feet) above sea level.

The town was established by the British in the early 19th century as a summer retreat. John Sullivan, a British administrator, is credited with discovering Ooty in 1819 and building the first bungalow here in 1822. The British developed the town extensively, building churches, schools, and botanical gardens that still stand today.

## Climate

Ooty has a pleasant subtropical highland climate:

- **Summer (March-May)**: 10°C to 25°C - Most pleasant time with clear skies
- **Monsoon (June-September)**: 12°C to 20°C - Heavy rainfall, especially July-August
- **Winter (October-February)**: 5°C to 18°C - Cold mornings, occasional frost

The weather can change rapidly. Always carry layers regardless of the season. Morning fog is common throughout the year.

## Getting There

### By Air
Coimbatore International Airport is 88 km away. Taxis cost around Rs 2,000-2,500 for the journey.

### By Train
The UNESCO World Heritage Nilgiri Mountain Railway connects Ooty to Mettupalayam. The toy train journey takes about 5 hours and offers spectacular views of the mountains, forests, and tea gardens. Booking in advance through IRCTC is essential, especially during peak season.

### By Road
- Bangalore: 280 km (6-7 hours)
- Coimbatore: 86 km (2.5-3 hours)
- Mysore: 130 km (3-4 hours)
- Chennai: 550 km (10-12 hours)

## Getting Around

- **Taxis**: Most convenient for sightseeing. Full-day hire costs Rs 2,000-3,000
- **Auto-rickshaws**: Good for short distances. Always negotiate the fare beforehand
- **Local buses**: Connect major tourist spots but can be infrequent
- **Self-drive**: Not recommended for first-timers due to winding mountain roads

## Top Attractions

### Nature & Scenic Points

**Doddabetta Peak** - At 2,637 meters, this is the highest peak in the Nilgiris. The telescope house offers views on clear days extending to the Mysore plateau. Best visited early morning before the mist rolls in.

**Ooty Lake** - An artificial lake created by John Sullivan in 1824. Boating is available with pedal boats, row boats, and motor boats. The surrounding garden has a toy train for children.

**Pykara Falls** - Located 20 km from Ooty, these falls cascade through pine and eucalyptus forests. The adjacent Pykara Lake offers boating. Best during and after monsoon season.

**Avalanche Lake** - A pristine lake formed by a landslide in 1823. Known for trout fishing. Requires forest department permit. The approach road is rough.

### Gardens

**Government Botanical Gardens** - Established in 1848, these 22-hectare gardens house over 1,000 species of plants, including rare orchids and ferns. The fossil tree trunk is believed to be 20 million years old. Annual flower show held in May.

**Rose Garden** - One of India's largest rose gardens with 20,000+ varieties spread across terraced slopes. Best visited during peak blooming season (April-June).

### Heritage Sites

**St. Stephen's Church** - Built in 1829, one of the oldest churches in the Nilgiris. Features beautiful stained glass windows and colonial architecture.

**Stone House** - The first bungalow built in Ooty (1822) by John Sullivan. Now a heritage site.

**Nilgiri Mountain Railway** - A UNESCO World Heritage steam railway built in 1908. The scenic journey passes through 16 tunnels and over 250 bridges.

### Museums

**Tribal Research Centre Museum** - Learn about the Toda, Badaga, and other indigenous tribes of the Nilgiris. Small but informative collection.

**Thread Garden** - A unique attraction where all flowers are made from thread. Guinness World Record holder.

## Where to Eat

**Shinkow's** - Iconic Chinese restaurant since 1935. Famous for hot and sour soup and momos.

**Earl Grey Cafe** - Cozy cafe specializing in Nilgiri teas and continental breakfast.

**Sidewalk Cafe** - Popular vegetarian restaurant serving Italian and continental dishes.

**Adderley Tea Room** - Heritage tea room for traditional English high tea experience.

## What to Buy

- **Nilgiri Tea**: Orthodox, green, and flavored varieties from local estates
- **Homemade Chocolates**: From shops like Kingstar and Moddy's
- **Eucalyptus Oil**: Good for cold relief, available at government outlets
- **Varkey**: Local butter biscuits, perfect with tea
- **Nilgiri Honey**: Pure honey from local apiaries

## Tips for Visitors

1. **Book accommodations in advance** during peak season (March-June)
2. **Start early** for outdoor sightseeing before afternoon mist
3. **Carry cash** as many places don't accept cards
4. **Respect local customs** especially at tribal areas
5. **Don't litter** - Ooty is eco-sensitive
6. **Drive carefully** on winding mountain roads
7. **Book toy train tickets** at least a week in advance

## Day Trips from Ooty

**Coonoor** (19 km) - The second largest town in the Nilgiris. Visit Sim's Park, Dolphin's Nose viewpoint, and Lamb's Rock.

**Kotagiri** (28 km) - Quieter hill station with colonial bungalows and viewpoints.

**Mudumalai National Park** (36 km) - Wildlife sanctuary home to elephants, tigers, and various deer species.

## Safety

Ooty is generally safe for all travelers. Take normal precautions:
- Use registered taxis or hotel transport
- Be careful on mountain roads, especially during monsoon
- Stay on marked trails while trekking
- Carry basic medications as remote areas lack pharmacies
